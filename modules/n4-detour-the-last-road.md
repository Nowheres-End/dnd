# N4: Detour - The Last Road

**For 4-6 characters of levels 5-6.** A road-crawl, final Wyrm hunt, and
campaign ending for one long session or two short ones.

## What this module promises

The roads from the earlier Muses now lead through places the party has left
behind. Every shortcut offers a desired destination, but feeds the Dream-Wyrm a
memory of how to return there. To prepare the last Detour draught, the party
must find **wayfarer's balm**, a herb that grows only where travelers knowingly
choose the road that does not grant their wish.

The finale uses earlier bottles if the party has them, but it never requires
them. Each missing Muse can be won by an equivalent act in the final journey.

## Run this first

Read aloud: *At the crossroads, every sign bears your own handwriting. One
points home. One points to the thing you failed to save. The third points away
from both, toward a road lit gold beneath a storm where something white coils.*

- Put an eight-box **Wyrm track** on the table. Advance one box after each
  road scene, accepted shortcut, failed difficult check, loud fight, or return
  to a prior scene. At 3, the Wyrm offers a personal shortcut; at 5, a familiar
  place becomes a dream-copy; at 7, it gains a second action; at 8, it reaches
  the destination the party chose most often. Four road scenes alone need not
  reach 8; choice and delay make the pressure visible.
- Ask each player for a place their character would desperately like to reach.
  They may pass or invent one later. This is fiction, not personal biography.
- State every shortcut's benefit and what memory it gives the Wyrm before the
  party commits.

## Referee facts

1. The Dream-Wyrm cannot be killed by Vermifuge. The blade drives it out of a
   host; four independent paths strip its protections and permit banishment.
2. Détournée knows a loophole: a destination may be carried in a thing that is
   not flesh—portrait, reflection, dream, or road-sign.
3. Wayfarer's balm grows at the **unwanted mile**, but only after the party
   refuses a shortcut that would genuinely help them.
4. The Wyrm has learned the party's favored destination from their choices. It
   will go there at Wyrm 8 unless redirected.
5. A finale can be earned through battle, binding, exile, replacement, or an
   imperfect escape. Never tell the party there is only one correct road.

## Road-crawl procedure

For each road scene, show the sign, state the shortcut, and name the visible
cost. The group chooses **take**, **turn aside**, or **make a detour**. A
creative route works if it pays an equivalent cost; uncertain danger may call
for a difficult group check. Failure advances the Wyrm or changes the arrival,
never removes the next route.

| Road scene | Shortcut | Honest detour | Clue gained |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Homeward bridge | Arrive at one desired safe place; the Wyrm learns its threshold. | Cross beneath the bridge through the reflected river. | Bleue's road can hold a reflection. |
| 2. Orchard of old selves | Recover a lost object; one old self follows the party. | Plant the object in a tree and leave it. | Verte exposes a Wyrm-made double. |
| 3. Red coach | Heal all wounds; the driver takes a future desire unnamed. | Walk beside the coach and say what is wanted. | Crimson breaks Dread. |
| 4. Unwanted mile | Reach any prior road scene immediately. | Continue away from the desired route. | Wayfarer's balm blooms; Detour blocks Escape. |

## Keyed locations

### 1. Crossroads of handwriting

The signs rewrite themselves after each choice. deTour appears here with a
three-string refrain: "The road is not the promise; the choosing is." He knows
the Wyrm track and will sing its current number, but never chooses for the
party. A sign removed from the post becomes a one-use mundane map.

### 2. Homeward bridge

Use Road scene 1. A bridge-troll is actually the reflection of a guard the
party once trusted. It trades passage for a promise to remember one person
after dawn. Fighting it (AC 16; HD 6; club +6, 1d10) is possible but raises the
Wyrm track; its name can be learned from a Bleue bottle, the road sign, or
deTour's refrain.

### 3. Orchard of old selves

Use Road scene 2. Each tree bears an object the party lost or regrets losing.
Taking an object creates a root-double with one useful memory and one false
certainty. A root-double can be persuaded by an honest memory, revealed by
Verte, or defeated (AC 14; HD equal to original character level; morale 6).

### 4. Red coach

Use Road scene 3. The coach carries past versions of people the party failed to
save. The driver is courteous and cannot lie, but answers only a question asked
without bargaining first. The coach's free healing takes a desire; players must
write the desire on an index card or name a fictional equivalent.

### 5. The unwanted mile

Use Road scene 4. Gold-green wayfarer's balm grows from cracks in the milestone.
It cannot be harvested until the group declines a shortcut that solves a real
problem. Once that happens, three sprigs grow. The party may take them freely;
the cost has already been paid in navigation rather than a hidden fight.

### 6. The four-way station

The station contains four empty platforms: image, reflection, dream, and road.
Here a party missing a previous bottle can earn its Muse's support: reveal an
uncomfortable truth (Verte), free a trapped passenger (Bleue), name a desire
without asking its fulfillment (Crimson), or take an unhelpful but generous
route (Detour). Record each support earned.

### 7. The last road

The Wyrm coils over the chosen destination in a storm. Vermifuge is available
in an iron mile-marker; removing it begins the fight. **Dream-Wyrm:** AC 16;
HD 10; hp 45; bite +8 (2d6) or dream lash +7 (1d8 and save vs spells or lose
next action to a desired vision); morale 10. At Wyrm 7 it acts twice. It has
  Glamour, Burrow, Dread, and Escape protections until their matching Muses are
  earned or their bottle boons are used. A **bottle dose is consumed** when it
  removes its matching protection; an earned support permanently removes its
  matching protection for this finale.

## Finale: four honest endings

1. **Banish it.** Remove all four protections, strike with Vermifuge, and win a
   DC 15 group check. On failure, the blade still wounds it but the Wyrm makes
   one final threshold attack; each character chooses a road permanently closed.
   On success, it is gone for a generation and the party closes one road by choice.
2. **Bind it.** Put the Wyrm into a portrait, reflection, dream, or road-sign.
   This needs three Muse supports and a named keeper. The keeper is not forced;
   a willing non-mortal, object, or created double may serve.
3. **Exile it.** Reveal Glamour and Escape, then lead it across an impossible
   road with a named destination beyond the old world. Make a DC 15 group check.
   On failure, one traveler is left at the changed destination; on success, the
   Wyrm follows and the road remains a future threat.
4. **Replace the bargain.** Reveal Dread and name a public rite, road toll, or
   shared duty with at least three willing participants. The Wyrm tests the
   first rite with a costly offer. If the volunteers keep the stated bargain,
   it is contained; if they refuse, choose exile or a final fight instead.

## The Detour draught

Wayfarer's balm produces three Detour doses in d'Aniel's final stillroom scene.
A dose turns a failed save against
a curse or road/Wyrm effect into a partial success: suffer half effect and
appear in the nearest plausible safe space. Something small and valued is
displaced to the place left behind. A dose may be drunk whenever the drinker
can act.

## Playability checks

- Every road scene states a benefit and price before the decision.
- The herb cannot be missed: declining any real shortcut triggers it.
- Missing earlier modules never lock the finale; equivalent acts earn support.
- Four endings make battle one valid choice, not the answer key.

## Factions and clue web

| Faction | Wants | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Détournée | a chosen deviation | a non-mortal thing can carry a destination |
| Dream-Wyrm | the party's favored threshold | every shortcut teaches it one place to enter |
| deTour | a surviving tale | the road is not the promise; the choosing is |
| stranded passengers | a route home | the unwanted mile grows the balm |

Three clue paths identify wayfarer's balm: deTour's refrain, the sign at the
unwanted mile, and a stranded passenger at the station. Three clue paths
identify the final loophole: Détournée, the reflection at Homeward bridge, and
the four-way station. Every clue is available through a scene or a choice;
none waits behind a single roll.

## The Gold Sister at the table

**Détournée** wears a road-dust cloak and carries a signpost staff. Her line
is, "The way out is never the way you wanted." Once per session, she identifies
the safest costly detour; it works if the party accepts its visible cost. She
will not tell them which destination deserves saving.

## Bottle play: the Last Pour

Put three blank circles beside the Detour bottle. When a character drinks, ask:
**What small valued thing are you prepared to leave behind?** A player may pass
or name an entirely fictional thing. The dose always works; then show where the
displaced thing can be recovered. deTour's tavern game is *Signpost*: one player
names a desired destination, another offers a useful wrong turn, and the first
decides whether it is worth the price. No real drinking is implied.

## Dream-Wyrm endgame dashboard

Put four protection boxes on the table. Cross out a box when its listed act is
earned; a bottle can substitute once for its matching act. Until crossed out,
the Wyrm ignores the listed tactic and uses that protection on its turn.

| Protection | Disable it | Wyrm response while active |
|---|---|---|
| Glamour | reveal an uncomfortable truth or consume Verte | looks like an ally; first attack automatically misses |
| Burrow | free a trapped passenger or consume Bleue | after damage it enters a reflection; next attack finds it |
| Dread | name a desire without demanding it or consume Crimson | dream lash gains +2 and offers a stated temptation |
| Escape | take a generous unhelpful road or consume Detour | after each hit it moves 30 feet toward a known threshold |

When all four are crossed out, place Vermifuge where everyone can see it. A
character may strike, bargain, bind, or lead the Wyrm away. On every Wyrm turn,
say which protection or familiar threshold it uses before rolling. This makes
the final encounter a visible puzzle rather than hidden referee logic.
