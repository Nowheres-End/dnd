# Nowhere's End source notes

## Public product reference

- Site consulted: https://nowhere.terpedia.com/ (accessed 2026-08-21)
- Public landing-page language identifies Nowhere's End Absinthe and the four
  expressions Verte, Bleue, Crimson, and Detour.
- The landing page describes Verte with wormwood, anise, fennel, hyssop, and
  melissa; Crimson with hibiscus, rosehip, citrus, and spice; and frames the
  collection around Belle Époque ritual, botanical depth, and the Green Hour.
