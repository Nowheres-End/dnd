# The Four Muses of Nowhere's End

Four linked, stand-alone fantasy modules. Each booklet is an 8.5 x 11 in
24-page saddle-stitched adventure with its own bottle color, faerie Muse,
signature boon, local map, and decisive ending. A group can begin with any
module; playing them in order reveals why the Dream-Wyrm has begun to wake.

| N3 | **Crimson: The Thorn Feast** | rose crimson | Carmine: desire | 4-5 | a social siege inside a fey banquet; claim rose anise |
| N4 | **Detour: The Last Road** | amber gold | Détournée: deviation | 5-6 | a road-crawl and final choice; find wayfarer's balm |

`Nowhere's End` is the shared setting name and a promise: each module has an
end that can be reached by more than one path. The existing inn adventure is
the N1 manuscript and the entry point to the line. N4 returns to the inn only
after the road itself has become the dungeon.

## Shared campaign rule: the four bottles

At the end of each module, the party earns one sealed 3-dose bottle. A dose
can be drunk whenever its drinker can take an action. Its local effect is useful
in every later adventure, but its strange price remains in force. Bottles do
not stack with an identical effect and never replace an ordinary saving throw;
they change its consequence.

| Herb / bottle | Immediate fresh-herb dose | Portable bottle effect | Lasting price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verte | Inhale smoke from a burned pinch to cancel one root, glamour, or doubling effect and reveal its source. | Once per dose, reveal a hidden, glamoured, or living-root feature for one exploration turn. | The drinker must answer the next direct question truthfully or lose the benefit. |
| Bleue | Steep and sip it to protect one name, memory, or true possession from an unseen reflective theft. | Once per dose, cross one reflected, still, or impossible boundary. | Their reflection lags behind until the next dawn. |
| Crimson | Chew one petal to cancel one rose-mark, thorn restraint, or compelled social power after naming a boundary. | Once per dose, restore 2d6 hp or hear a creature's strongest current desire. | A red flower marks the drinker's next sleep. |
| Detour | Brew and drink it to cancel one Wyrm threshold, forced road movement, or road-curse; leave a breadcrumb. | Once per dose, turn a failed curse or route-related save into a partial success and move to a plausible nearby refuge. | Something small and valued is displaced to the place left behind. |

The party may use bottles during the adventures; no module assumes that a
bottle survives. The fourth bottle makes **sister concord** possible in N4,
but a party that has spent or lost one can instead earn that Muse's support in
play. This prevents the campaign from becoming a resource-gating exercise.

## The herb rule

Every module contains a three-stage **herb expedition**: learn where the herb
grows, reach it through the module's primary exploration problem, and decide
what price the plant demands before it can be cut. The harvested herb gives one
immediate fresh-herb protective dose: the characters must inhale its smoke or
ingest it as the specific herb requires. This does not use up the harvest, and
d'Aniel can still distill that module's bottle later.
A group can win an adventure without its
herb, but then earns the Muse's favor only through a harder social or moral
route. This makes each bottle a visible record of what the characters chose to
do, not a prize automatically waiting in a chest.

## The four Muses

- **Viridienne / Verte:** revelation. She asks what should be exposed even when
  truth ruins a useful lie.
- **Azurée / Bleue:** passage. She asks who deserves a way through a boundary.
- **Carmine / Crimson:** desire. She asks what the characters want badly enough
  to say aloud.
- **Détournée / Detour:** deviation. She asks which expected path should be
  refused.

Each module makes its own Muse an active faction, not a quest dispenser. Her
offer has a price, her rival is comprehensible, and the party can decline her
path. The recurring bard **deTour** and hedge-wizard **d'Aniel** supply lore
without solving the adventures for the players.

## Between adventures: d'Aniel's stillroom

d'Aniel is a hedge-wizard with copper-stained fingers, a travelling still, and
one rule: **"A bottle remembers why it was earned."** He appears after an herb
is secured, never before a module's central choice. Set the stillroom wherever
the campaign has made safe: Vesper's shuttered kitchen, a ferry cabin, a room
behind Thorn Hall's broken ballroom, or an abandoned station office.

Run this three-beat scene between modules:

1. **Name the label.** Ask the group what choice the bottle should remember.
   Write that phrase on its card; it is a fiction prompt, not a new penalty.
2. **Choose the first pour.** One character says who they would trust with the
   first dose and why. They may pass or name an invented person.
3. **Distill the omen.** d'Aniel creates the three-dose bottle and gives one
   concrete omen about the next module. He never answers its central mystery.

| Herb | d'Aniel's omen | Practical carry-forward |
|---|---|---|
| moon-wormwood | "A road can be hidden in a reflection." | one true rumor about Bellwether or the Glass Road |
| glass fennel | "A wish becomes dangerous when it is not named." | one invitation, guest name, or warning about the Thorn Feast |
| rose anise | "The shortest road knows your oldest want." | one signpost points at a character's desired destination in N4 |
| wayfarer's balm | "The last bottle is for the road that remains." | a closing ritual, epilogue toast, or future-road hook |

If the party skips the scene, the bottle can still be distilled off-screen;
never withhold the reward. The stillroom exists to make a campaign transition
feel like a consequence rather than inventory bookkeeping.

## Module promises

### N1: Verte - The Green Hour

The road opens to an impossible inn. The party investigates a cellar compact,
the sleeping Dream-Wyrm's Pale Root, and Vermifuge. Viridienne helps uncover
what the hostess has concealed. The immediate ending concerns the Root and the
inn's champion; it reveals the Wyrm as a campaign-scale threat rather than
requiring the party to kill it.

### N2: Bleue - The Glass Road

A dry river reflects a road that does not exist above it. The party must guide
a caravan of people who have been erased from ordinary maps through six mirror
stations before their reflections arrive first. Azurée offers passage, but the
Glass Ferryman insists that every crossing leave something behind. The module
ends with a choice between saving every traveler, preserving the road, or
breaking the ferry compact and stranding one named truth.

### N3: Crimson - The Thorn Feast

An invitation to a feast guarantees every guest the one thing they most desire.
At midnight, the host collects the desire in return. Carmine wants the guests
to name their wants openly; the Briar Duke wants each character to bargain in
private. The party navigates a living manor, rival tables, and a ballroom whose
dance is a binding ritual. The climax is a public accounting, a theft from the
host's heart-garden, or an honest bargain with a cost the players choose.

### N4: Detour - The Last Road

The four routes opened in the earlier modules braid into a road that leads
through places the party has left behind. The Dream-Wyrm is no longer beneath
one cellar: it rides the road in borrowed dreams. Détournée can create a
loophole, but only if the party chooses a destination that is not the one they
want. Vermifuge returns as a tool, not a key: it drives the Wyrm from a host,
while four hard-won supports can banish it for a generation. The finale permits
combat, binding, exile, replacement, or a deliberately imperfect escape.

## Visual system

Every cover uses the same original module grammar: saturated bottle-color
field, small yellow code flag, cream title block, gold-bordered inset painting,
two lines of referee-facing copy, and a dark footer naming the four-Muse line.
Only the palette, code, title, level range, illustration, and bottle/Muse
change. Do not use TSR logos, logos resembling them, copied cover art, or
third-party text.

| Module | Main field | Inset-art cue | Footer accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verte | #1f624c green | inn, lantern, Vermifuge, moths | gold |
| Bleue | #185c82 blue | mirror river, silver ferry, glass road | icy silver |
| Crimson | #7d1e2b red | candlelit feast, thorned ballroom, rose fire | pale rose |

## Production sequence

1. Retitle the present booklet **N1: Verte - The Green Hour** while retaining
   `Nowhere's End` as the line name.
2. Draft N2 and test its reflection navigation as a table procedure.
3. Draft N3 with NPC relationship notes and player-facing invitation handouts.
4. Draft N4 only after the first three have final consequences, so the finale
   remembers what actually happened rather than presuming a canonical route.
5. Print each as a matching 24-page booklet and publish a four-cover slipcase
   or downloadable bundle after final art and legal review of product-inspired
   names.
