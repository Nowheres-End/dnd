# Hand-print imposition — 24-page saddle stitch

Use this only if the printer asks for imposed spreads. Each row is one side of
a physical sheet; numbers read left page | right page.

| Sheet (outside → inside) | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 24 \| 1 | 2 \| 23 |
| 2 | 22 \| 3 | 4 \| 21 |
| 3 | 20 \| 5 | 6 \| 19 |
| 4 | 18 \| 7 | 8 \| 17 |
| 5 | 16 \| 9 | 10 \| 15 |
| 6 | 14 \| 11 | 12 \| 13 |

Print duplex with short-edge binding, fold all six sheets together, then staple
twice along the fold. Test the imposition on plain paper first.
