# Preflight checklist

## Content

- [ ] Final page count is exactly divisible by four.
- [ ] Contents and page references match the exported PDF.
- [ ] Every map key, potion effect, and boxed text is readable at actual size.
- [ ] Product references and trademark permissions have been reviewed.
- [ ] Credits, license, and contact/publisher information are present.

## Production

- [ ] Finished trim is 8.5 × 11 in, portrait—not a half-letter booklet.
- [ ] Bleed extends 0.125 in beyond trim wherever an image meets the edge.
- [ ] All live text stays inside the safe area.
- [ ] Images are at least 300 ppi at final placement size.
- [ ] Black body text is set to 100K, not rich black.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded and overprint settings have been checked.
- [ ] A physical double-sided proof has been folded and stapled.
- [ ] The proof has no reversed spreads, hidden gutter text, or crop/bleed gaps.
