# Referee procedures

## The Green Hour

Begin after the clock's thirteenth stroke. Each exploration turn, roll 1d6:

| d6 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | All lamps turn green; a hidden route becomes visible. |
| 2 | 1d4 mothlings arrive at the noisiest location. |
| 3 | One guest repeats a memory that contains a clue. |
| 4 | The Pale Root makes an offer to the most isolated character. |
| 5 | A clock elsewhere chimes; everyone loses one minute, harmlessly but noticeably. |
| 6 | Choose or repeat a result; the inn shifts one room's exit. |

## Fair bargain procedure

When a fairy offers terms, write the exact sentence down. A bargain binds only
when all parties repeat it, accept a gift, or cross a threshold after hearing
it. Reward literal cleverness. Do not hide the wording from attentive players.

## Finale procedure

Use this procedure whenever Vermifuge leaves the chair or the Root-heart reaches
0 hp. Put the sword where everyone can see it, name its bearer, and reveal the
Dream-Wyrm. It takes one action per round before Green Hour 6 and two thereafter.

1. **Name the ward.** Read the compact aloud. The party may offer a legal
   substitute through sister concord before fighting.
2. **Run the protections.** Apply Glamour, Burrow, Dread, and Escape. A matching
   Last Pour boon removes its protection as a free declaration after that effect.
   A non-potion answer also exists, once per protection: spend an action to
   speak a verifiable truth into bright green lamplight (Glamour ends for the
   scene); spend an action to press the silver key into a closed iron ring
   (Burrow ends until the Wyrm's next turn); spend an action to name a cherished
   in-world memory and take 1 hp damage (Dread ends for that character); or
   spend an action to shatter a mirror, polished shield, or still-water surface
   within reach (Escape ends for the scene). These answers are riskier and
   temporary, but no party is locked out.
3. **Resolve Vermifuge.** With any protection remaining, reducing the Wyrm to
   0 hp forces it below the moor until the next Green Hour. With all four gone,
   Vermifuge deals 2d6 and forces a save vs. spells; failure banishes it for a
   generation.

## End states

**The hard-won cut.** The Wyrm is banished for a generation. Vesper ages a
century overnight and is free; the inn remains, altered but honest.

**The renewed ward.** Sister concord accepts a portrait, reflection, dream, or
non-mortal bearer. The Court renews the prison and leaves a future favor owing.
Vesper remains keeper, but no living character is sacrificed.

**The open road.** The party carries Vermifuge away. Nowhere's End vanishes at
dawn; the Wyrm is not solved, and roads across the region begin leading where
they should not. This is an excellent campaign continuation.
