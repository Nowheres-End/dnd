# Player introduction

Read or paraphrase:

> The road sheds its ruts behind you. Ahead, a warm lamp burns above a door
> set in open moorland. The windows show a crowded common room, though no
> smoke rises from the chimney. Above the door is no name—only a small green
> moth, circling a clock whose hands have stopped one minute before midnight.
>
> As you draw close, the door opens. A woman in mourning silk smiles without
> surprise. “Travelers,” she says, “you are precisely in time for the Green
> Hour. Do try not to promise anything.”

## What players can immediately see

- The inn has a common room, bar, upstairs gallery, kitchen door, and a
  padlocked cellar hatch behind the bar.
- Seven guests sit too quietly around a long table. One has leaves in her hair.
- A pale young traveler in ash-gray clothes watches the cellar hatch.
- There is no road visible behind the party once they cross the threshold.
