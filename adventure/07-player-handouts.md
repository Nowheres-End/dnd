# Player handouts

## The compact

Give this text to the players when they find Vesper’s ledger, ask the third
portrait the right question, or receive it as a bargain from Lark.

> A mortal champion bearing the blade shall come freely to the Court of Moths
> at the last stroke of the Green Hour. In exchange, the road shall remain open,
> the inn shall endure, and no root shall drink the dreams of those beneath its
> roof.

## Lark’s riddle

> “A picture bears a face. A dream bears a sleeper. A mirror bears a traveler.
> Tell me, oath-breaker: which of these must bleed?”

## Potion labels

| Bottle | Label |
|---|---|

| Bleue | *For taking the road held in glass.* |

| Detour | *For reaching the place you should not reach.* |

## Four paths card

Give these four lines to the players when the first sister appears:

> Green reveals. Blue carries. Red desires. Gold strays.
>
> Four sisters, four cups, four ways to reach the end of the road.

## The Last Pour

Give this to the table when Vesper brings four glasses:

> Name what you seek. Choose a different color. Then speak, in turn: a question
> you have avoided; a road you would take; a desire you would admit; a wrong
> turn you would choose again. Do not laugh. Do not lie. Drink together. Let
> the vermifuge drive the Wyrm from your heart.
