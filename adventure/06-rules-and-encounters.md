# Rules, encounters, and escalation

This module uses a compact old-school chassis: ascending Armor Class, Hit Dice
(d8), morale, and saves. It runs directly in most B/X-derived games; for other
systems, use the listed HD and intent rather than translating every number.

## Table rules

- **Checks:** Roll d20, succeed on 12+; use 10+ for a sensible plan and 15+ for
  a difficult or dangerous one. A relevant class ability may succeed outright.
- **Turns:** One exploration turn is roughly ten minutes. Searching a room,
  treating an injury, or navigating the cask vault consumes one turn.
- **Reaction:** On first contact with an undecided creature, roll 2d6: 2 hostile,
  3-5 unfriendly, 6-8 uncertain, 9-11 favorable, 12 helpful. Roleplay can alter
  the result.
- **Morale:** Creatures check morale at half their number or hp, and when their
  leader falls; roll 2d6 over their morale to flee, bargain, or surrender.

**Save conversion.** “Save vs. spells” means the system's magic, will, or
mental-effect save; “save vs. paralysis” means its restraint, body, or
fortitude save. “Save as [level]” means use the listed character level's normal
saving throw progression. If your game has no saves, use a difficult check.

## The thirteenth stroke

When the party has spent ten minutes in the inn—or when they force the clock—
read: *The second hand moves. The clock chimes once, twice, and then thirteen
times. With the last note, every lamp burns green.* Begin at Green Hour 1.

| Green Hour | Change | Referee action |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Glamours thin. | Reveal one hidden route or clue. The hatch opens. |
| 2 | Guests dream awake. | Nella becomes a root-double unless protected. |
| 3 | The Court arrives. | Lark demands a decision and mothlings patrol. |
| 4 | The Root drinks deeply. | Each isolated sleeper/distracted PC receives an offer. |
| 5 | The inn begins to fail. | Fire, violence, or broken casks collapse one exit. |
| 6 | Dawn presses at the windows. | The Root gains a second action; resolve the finale now. |

After each exploration turn, also roll the Green Hour event table in
`05-referee-procedures.md` if the party has not already triggered a major event.

## Encounters

### Madame Vesper

AC 13; HD 4; hp 18; Move 30; Attack shadow lash +4 (1d6, target cannot cross a
threshold next round); Save as 4th-level magic-user; Morale 8. She does not
fight to kill. At 0 hp she dissolves into the nearest shadow and returns at
dawn if the inn remains. **Tactics:** offers a bargain first; protects the inn
over any individual; breaks off combat if Vermifuge is drawn against the Root.

### Lark-of-Ash

AC 14; HD 3; hp 13; Move 40/fly 60 as moth; Attack thorn knife +3 (1d4) or
glamour (save vs. spells or lose next action admiring a false path); Save as
3rd-level thief; Morale 10. Lark cannot knowingly speak a direct lie and will
not break a completed bargain. **Tactics:** avoids combat; bargains for the
sword; reveals the silver key when treated with formal courtesy.

### Mothling swarm

AC 12; HD 2; hp 9; Fly 30; Attack cloud (all in a 10-foot space take 1d4 and
must save vs. spells or lose sight of fine detail for one turn); Morale 7.
Normal weapons deal half damage; fire deals double damage but risks the inn.
Loud, unmetered music drives a swarm away for 1d6 turns.

### Root-double

AC 12; HD 2; hp 10; Move 30; Attack root-hand +2 (1d6); Morale 9. It knows the
victim’s memories through the previous dawn and speaks in their voice. A
root-double hesitates for one round when shown a cherished object from its
original. Vermifuge kills it in one strike; fire harms both double and inn.

### Dream-Wyrm (final form)

AC 15; HD 6; hp 27; Move 20/burrow 20; bite +5 (2d6 plus save vs. spells or
dream one round); Morale 11. It rises when the Root-heart is destroyed or
Vermifuge leaves the chair. It takes a second action at Green Hour 6 or after
three Root offers are accepted. It begins with the following protections.

| Protection | Effect | Vermifuge boon that removes it |
|---|---|---|
| Glamour | It creates false exits and one root-double each round. | Verte turns a failed glamour/offer save into success. |
| Burrow | It may vanish below the floor after being hit. | Bleue succeeds against its roots and holds it in the chamber. |
| Dread | On appearing, all who see it save vs. spells or lose one round. | Crimson rerolls the fear or vitality save, breaking its hold. |
| Escape | At 0 hp, it retreats into a dream or mirror instead of withdrawing. | Detour prevents that route and forces the final Vermifuge save. |

Spending a matching boon is a free declaration immediately after the relevant
effect. The party may also use the non-potion counters in
`05-referee-procedures.md`, but those only suppress a protection for the scene.
With any protection remaining, reducing the Wyrm to 0 hp sends it below until
the next Green Hour. When all four are gone, Vermifuge deals 2d6 and forces a
save vs. spells; failure sends it below the moor for a generation. This makes
the Last Pour the cleanest route to a permanent victory, not a requirement for
survival.

## The Root’s offers

When the Root speaks, make the offer useful and exact a future price. Choose or
roll: (1) show the shortest route to Vermifuge; forget the face of a loved one
until dawn; (2) heal 2d6 hp; a white flower grows from the healed wound; (3)
reveal who will betray the party; the character cannot speak that person’s name;
(4) restore a spent spell; the Root may use the spell’s sound once; (5) make
one locked door open; the next open door leads somewhere wrong; (6) let the
character ask a dead portrait a question; the portrait will name them to the
Court.

## Running the sisters

The sisters are fey powers, not bags of hit points. Treat a sister’s offer as a
fair bargain: it binds only when accepted, and accepting it gives the party her
vote at the finale. If attacked, a sister becomes a cloud of her color and
returns only after the next sunset; her bottle turns inert. This is a real cost:
the party loses both a powerful ally and the cleanest route to reinterpret the
compact.

## The tavern game

The Last Pour is a social encounter, not a puzzle with a single correct answer.
Ask each player for their chosen color and prompt in order: question, road,
desire, wrong turn. Let a player invent a fitting answer, or veil it; it need
only matter to their character. A complete sincere round gives all four sisters
a reason to appear; a veiled or evasive answer still activates the potion, but
leaves its sister unconvinced. Then give each sincere drinker the
matching one-use saving-throw boon from `04-treasure-and-potions.md`. Call out
that these are *vermifuges*: every boon has a direct use against the Pale Root,
its doubles, traps, curses, or compulsions. This makes drinking the draughts a
meaningful early choice instead of an endgame button.

## Finale procedure

When Vermifuge is taken, place the sword card on the table and ask who bears
it. The Root awakens and the Dream-Wyrm rises when the ward breaks. The party
can fight, bargain, ask the sisters to interpret the wording, or exploit it
without their aid. Follow `05-referee-procedures.md` for the exact resolution;
the loophole and Last Pour improve the ending, but neither is required to survive.
