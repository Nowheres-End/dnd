# N1: Nowhere's End

**An adventure for 4–6 characters of 2nd–3rd level**

> Beyond the final milestone stands an inn with no signboard, a cellar door
> without a key, and a clock that strikes thirteen at the Green Hour.

## Adventure at a glance

The immortal hostess, **Madame Vesper**, has kept Nowhere's End open through a
compact with the Court of Moths. Her safeguard, the sword Vermifuge, kills the
white root that feeds on dreams - the feeding tendril of an ancient **Dream-Wyrm**
coiled beneath the moor. The sword has awakened; its call has caused
the Green Hour to spill through the inn and into the nearby world. Four faerie
sisters—the keepers of Verte, Bleue, Crimson, and Detour—have been called to
the inn by the same disturbance.

The characters arrive as a fairy emissary demands the blade. The inn's guests
are already changing. To end the spill, the party must descend to the cellar,
learn the terms of the compact, and choose an ending.

The tale is commonly told by **deTour**, a road-worn bard who insists he was
there and changes one small detail with every performance. The draughts were
concocted by **d'Aniel**, a hedge-wizard and former guest of Vesper's; their
four bottles are the only part of his unfinished remedy that remains at the inn.

## Referee facts

- Vermifuge is not cursed; it was forged to drive the Dream-Wyrm from a host.
  The *Pale Root* is its parasitic feeding tendril beneath the cellar. Its
  wielder hears the wyrm as a dry whisper.
- Madame Vesper promised the Court one willing mortal champion in every century.
  The champion bears Vermifuge at the Green Hour and renews the ward; the oath
  prevents the Wyrm from feeding on an innocent dreamer. Vesper intends to
  offer the bearer of Vermifuge, whether they understand the price or not.
- The emissary, Lark-of-Ash, wants the Root destroyed, but cannot say so while
  bound by Court etiquette.
- Each Green Hour potion reveals one truth and exacts one strange price.
- Each sister holds one path of the old compact. Their agreement can rewrite
  the bargain; their division lets the Pale Root endure.
- The Court bound the Dream-Wyrm beneath the inn long ago. Vesper’s compact
  renews that prison without feeding the Wyrm another innocent dreamer. The
  Court prefers the bargain to risking a direct battle with the Wyrm.

## What makes the adventure move

The party begins with three visible pressures: Nella is turning into a root
double, Lark demands Vermifuge, and the exit-road has vanished. The clock
strikes thirteen ten minutes after the party enters unless they deliberately
interfere with it. After that, use the Green Hour track: the inn becomes more
dangerous, but clues and routes become more available. No single clue is
required: every major fact has at least three sources.

## Factions

| Faction | Wants | Will offer |
|---|---|---|
| Madame Vesper | The compact preserved | lodging, potion lore, half-truths |
| Court of Moths | Vermifuge returned | safe passage, fairy favors |
| Dream-Wyrm | a dreamer to take root | visions, false exits, obedient guests |
| Lark-of-Ash | the Root ended quietly | a silver key, a loophole |
| deTour, the bard | a tale worth surviving | rumors, a refrain, a remembered route |
| d'Aniel, hedge-wizard | his remedy completed | potion lore, a warding formula |
| The four sisters | their chosen path honored | a draught, a clue, a fey boon |

## Wyrm foreshadowing

Show at least two before the root-chamber: Nella dreams of a pearl-black eye
under the inn; Vermifuge whispers “the worm sleeps below”; the well answers
“Dreams, first; the Wyrm, last”; and a faded portrait shows a keeper holding a
sword over a great serpent-shaped shadow. The Pale Root is never the whole
enemy: it is the Dream-Wyrm’s exposed feeding organ.

## Suggested flow

1. Read the player introduction as the party reaches the inn.
2. Let them explore the common room and take sides—or refuse all sides.
3. At the thirteenth stroke, begin the Green Hour turn procedure.
4. Use the cellar map/key as the exploration core.
5. Resolve the compact at the root-chamber. If the Root is cut, run the Wyrm
   finale procedure; otherwise resolve concord or the open road.

## Finale truth

There is no consequence-free clean cut. Removing Vermifuge or reducing the
Root-heart to 0 hp breaks Vesper's ward and calls the Dream-Wyrm into the
root-chamber. The Wyrm can be driven below temporarily with Vermifuge, but it
is banished for a generation only after all four Last Pour protections are
removed. Sister concord renews the ward through a portrait, reflection, dream,
or non-mortal bearer; the open road leaves the Wyrm as a future campaign threat.

## Clue web

| Truth | Three ways to learn it |
|---|---|
| Vesper owes the Court a champion | faded portrait, Vesper’s ledger, Lark’s wording |
| The Dream-Wyrm feeds through dreams | Nella, whispering well, Root offer |
| The compact turns on “bearing” | portrait question, moth-crown etiquette, Lark’s riddle |
| The service stair reaches the cellar | third portrait, kitchen automaton, upside-down chalk arrows |
| Vermifuge drives the Wyrm from a host | sword whisper, well, Vesper’s ledger |

## Adventure hooks

- A beloved NPC has sleepwalked down a road that only appears at dusk.
- A collector hires the party to authenticate Vermifuge.
- The first bottle of Verte has appeared in a local apothecary, sealed with a
  moth-shaped wax stamp.

## The Verte herb objective

N1 is the first herb expedition in the Four Muses line. The party can obtain
or replenish Verte only by reaching the Root-chamber and harvesting
moon-wormwood. It blooms through revelation or mercy, not a hidden skill roll:
ask a dreaded question aloud or free a root-double. The route has three clue
paths and the herb can be force-cut at a declared cost, so a party never loses
the adventure because it missed one room or failed one check.
